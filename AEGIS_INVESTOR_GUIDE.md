# Finding Investors for AEGIS 🎯

## Overview
This guide outlines where to find investors, how to approach them, and what materials to prepare.

---

## 🎯 Types of Investors to Target

### 1. Angel Investors
**Best for seed stage ($50K - $500K)**

**Where to Find:**
- **AngelList** (angel.co) - Create a startup profile
- **Gust** (gust.com) - Connect with angel networks
- **LinkedIn** - Search "angel investor" + "cybersecurity" or "mobile"
- **Angel Groups:**
  - Tech Coast Angels
  - Golden Seeds
  - Band of Angels
  - New York Angels
  - Australian Angel investors (if in AU)

**How to Approach:**
- Warm intros are best (ask your network)
- Cold email with concise pitch (see template below)
- Apply to angel group pitch events

---

## 🤝 Networking Strategies

### 1. Warm Introductions
- **Best success rate** (30-40% vs 1-2% cold)
- Ask: "Who do you know that invests in security/mobile?"
- Use LinkedIn to find 2nd connections
- Offer to help others first

### 2. Twitter/X
- Follow investors who tweet about security
- Engage genuinely (not just pitching)
- Share your building journey
- Tag investors thoughtfully

### 3. Events
- Attend startup/VC events
- Demo days (even as audience)
- Security conferences
- Don't pitch immediately - build relationships

### 4. Community
- Join Indie Hackers
- Reddit: r/startups, r/entrepreneur
- Hacker News (Show HN)
- Product Hunt launch

## 📅 Fundraising Timeline

### Typical Seed Round: 3-6 months

**Month 1: Preparation**
- Finalize pitch deck
- Build target investor list (50-100)
- Practice pitch
- Get warm intros lined up

**Month 2-3: Outreach**
- Send 10-20 emails per week
- Take all meetings offered
- Iterate on pitch based on feedback
- Apply to accelerators

**Month 4-5: Deep Conversations**
- Follow up with interested investors
- Share more materials
- Due diligence calls
- Term sheet negotiations

**Month 6: Close**
- Legal review
- Sign documents
- Money in bank!

## 💡 Tips for Success

### DO:
- ✅ Lead with the problem, not the solution
- ✅ Show, don't tell (demo the Trap Mode!)
- ✅ Know your numbers
- ✅ Be honest about stage/traction
- ✅ Follow up (but don't spam)
- ✅ Ask for intros even if they pass

### DON'T:
- ❌ Send deck without context
- ❌ Ask for NDA (red flag to investors)
- ❌ Overvalue your company
- ❌ Badmouth competitors
- ❌ Be desperate
- ❌ Take rejection personally

## 🎯 Ideal Investor Profile for AEGIS

Look for investors who:
- Have invested in **cybersecurity** or **mobile apps**
- Understand **consumer products**
- Are **patient** (consumer apps take time)
- Can add **strategic value** (introductions, advice)
- Are based in **your region** (or invest globally)
