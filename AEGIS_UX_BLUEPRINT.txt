CORE PHILOSOPHY: "COMPUTER BUTTER"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The interface should be:
    • SEAMLESS - No friction, no hunting
    • ADAPTIVE - Changes to fit your context
    • FRICTIONLESS - Everything in 1-2 taps

It should feel less like operating a tool and more like collaborating 
with a partner.

                    2. THE DAILY HUB
                   The "Contextual Home Screen"
================================================================================

CONCEPT:
    The traditional, static grid of app icons is GONE.
    The Aegis Hub is the user's new "home base."


LOOK & FEEL:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    • Dynamic, constantly changing dashboard
    • Feels ALIVE and RELEVANT
    • Not a list of apps - a list of ANSWERS and ACTIONS


HOW IT WORKS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    The Level 2 Manager AI directs this screen.
    
    It analyzes user context:
        • Time of day
        • Location
        • Calendar
        • Recent communications
    
    And re-prioritizes the interface accordingly.
    
    The screen is composed of modular "cards" or "widgets" that:
        • APPEAR when relevant
        • GROW when important
        • SHRINK when secondary
        • DISAPPEAR when not needed

                    4. ACCESSING SECRETS
                   The "Phantom Folder" Workflow
================================================================================

CONCEPT:
    The experience is designed to feel CLEVER, SECRET, and SECURE.


LOOK & FEEL:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    • Entry point is COMPLETELY MUNDANE (standard calculator)
    • The vault itself is clean, minimalist, and secure
    • Feels like having a secret room behind a bookshelf


HOW IT WORKS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    The "Secret Handshake" is a system-level hook managed by the Level 1 Owner.
    
    When the correct code is entered in the calculator, the Owner bypasses 
    the normal OS and opens the secure, encrypted partition.

    STEP 3: Press =

                    5. THE EMERGENCY EXPERIENCE
                  "Decoy Mode" & "Duress Protocol"
================================================================================

CONCEPT:
    The experience the user hopes to NEVER have, but is reassured knowing 
    it exists.


LOOK & FEEL:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    • Decoy Mode: Looks convincingly NORMAL but feels eerily EMPTY
    • Duress Protocol: Completely INVISIBLE


HOW IT WORKS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    DECOY MODE:
        Triggered automatically by the Behavioral Guard.
        The Level 2 Manager commands all Level 3 apps to serve 
        their "sanitized" state.

    DURESS PROTOCOL:
        Triggered by the Level 1 Owner's ambient listening model,
        which is always waiting for the Safe Word.


SCENARIO 1: THE SNOOPER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    1. User leaves their unlocked phone on the table
    
    2. A "friend" picks it up to snoop
    
    3. The Behavioral Guard detects: "This isn't the owner"
    
    4. Interface SUBTLY shifts to Decoy Mode
       (No visible alert - the snooper doesn't know)
    
    5. The snooper sees:
       • Generic messages
       • Bland photo gallery
       • No personal or sensitive data
    
    6. Snooper gives the phone back, thinking there's nothing interesting
    
    7. MEANWHILE, in the background:
       • Their photo was taken
       • Their actions were logged
       • Evidence file created
    
    8. Later, owner sees "Intruder Evidence" in their Security Log


SCENARIO 2: THE EMERGENCY (DURESS PROTOCOL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    1. User is in a dangerous situation
    
    2. User works their SAFE WORD into a sentence:
       "Hey Mate, can you call my lawyer?"
       (Sounds like a normal request)
    
    3. What the attacker sees:
       • Phone appears to make a normal call
       • Nothing suspicious
    
    4. What actually happens (SILENTLY):
       • Emergency services called
       • Location sent to trusted contacts
       • Audio recording begins
       • All evidence preserved
    
    5. The device is the user's silent, steadfast guardian
