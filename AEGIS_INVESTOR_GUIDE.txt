========================================
WHO TO TARGET
========================================

ANGEL INVESTORS
---------------
Best for: $50K - $500K

Where to find them:
- AngelList (angel.co) - make a profile
- Gust (gust.com) - connects you to angel networks
- LinkedIn - search "angel investor" + "cybersecurity" or "mobile"

Angel Groups worth looking at:
- Tech Coast Angels
- Golden Seeds  
- Band of Angels
- New York Angels
- Sydney Angels (local option)
- Melbourne Angels

How to approach:
- Warm intros work best (who do you know who knows them?)
- Cold email with a tight pitch (templates below)
- Apply to their pitch nights

========================================
NETWORKING TIPS
========================================

1. Warm Intros Are Gold
- 30-40% success rate vs 1-2% cold
- Ask everyone: "Who do you know that invests in security/mobile?"
- LinkedIn 2nd connections
- Help others first

2. Twitter/X
- Follow investors who talk about security
- Engage genuinely (not just pitching)
- Share your building journey

3. Events
- Go to startup events
- Demo days (even just as audience)
- Security conferences
- Build relationships before pitching

4. Communities
- Indie Hackers
- Reddit: r/startups, r/entrepreneur
- Hacker News (do a Show HN when ready)
- Product Hunt launch


========================================
TIMELINE
========================================

Typical seed round: 3-6 months

Month 1: Prep
- Finalize deck
- Build investor list (aim for 50-100 names)
- Practice your pitch
- Line up warm intros

Month 2-3: Outreach
- 10-20 emails per week
- Take every meeting
- Improve pitch based on feedback
- Apply to accelerators

Month 4-5: Deep Talks
- Follow up with interested ones
- Share more details
- Due diligence calls
- Negotiate terms

Month 6: Close
- Legal review
- Sign papers
- Money lands


========================================
DO'S AND DON'TS
========================================

DO:
- Lead with the problem
- Show, don't tell (demo Trap Mode!)
- Know your numbers
- Be honest about where you're at
- Follow up
- Ask for intros even if they say no

DON'T:
- Send deck without context
- Ask for NDA (red flag to investors)
- Overvalue yourself early on
- Badmouth competitors
- Act desperate
- Take rejection personally
