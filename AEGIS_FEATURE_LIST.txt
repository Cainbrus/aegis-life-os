AEGIS - What It Actually Does
================================
By Cain Brunjes | Digital Mate

The Simple Version
------------------
Aegis is your phone's bodyguard. But instead of just locking bad guys out, we let them IN - to fake stuff - while we grab their photo and location. They think they're in, but they've got nothing. That's the difference.


========================================
SECURITY STUFF
========================================

1. Three Different Unlock Patterns
----------------------------------
This is the core of Aegis:

Pattern                    | What Happens
---------------------------|------------------------------------------
Your Real Pattern          | Full access, everything's normal
Duress Pattern (someone's forcing you) | Shows fake data, secretly alerts your people
Wrong Pattern (intruder)   | Snaps their photo, shows them fake stuff

Simple as that. Three patterns, three different outcomes.


2. Trap Mode - This Is The Big One
----------------------------------
Every other security app locks people out. We don't.

We let them in. To fake data.

While they're looking at fake messages and fake photos thinking they've cracked it:
- We've taken their photo
- We've got their GPS location  
- We're logging everything they touch
- Your real stuff? Completely hidden

They walk away thinking they won. You've got evidence for the cops.

Nobody else does this. It's patentable.


3. Intruder Detection
---------------------
Someone tries to get into your phone:
- Wrong pattern 3 times = photo taken (they don't know)
- Location logged
- Time stamped
- You get a full report of what they tried to access


4. Remote Wipe
--------------
Phone's gone for good? Nuke it.
- Do it from any web browser
- Or set it to auto-wipe after X failed attempts
- Everything gone, confirmed


5. Calculator Vault
-------------------
Looks like a normal calculator. Type in 8675309 and boom - secret vault opens.
- Hide photos
- Hide documents  
- Hide whatever you need hidden
- In plain sight


========================================
EMERGENCY STUFF
========================================

1. Emergency Contacts
---------------------
- Add your people in order of priority
- Tag them (wife, mum, doctor, whatever)
- In an emergency, Aegis calls them one by one until someone picks up


2. Auto-Call 000
----------------
Configure it how you want:
- Auto-call emergency services in certain situations
- Or disable it if you'd rather handle it yourself
- Sends your location to responders


3. Crash Detection
------------------
Car accident? Phone detects the impact.
- Alerts your emergency contacts automatically
- Shares your GPS
- Gives you a countdown to cancel (in case it's a false alarm)


4. Shake SOS
------------
In trouble but can't get to your phone properly?
- Shake it 5 times fast
- Silent alert goes to your contacts
- Works from lock screen
- Nobody around you knows you've done it


5. Duress Phrase
----------------
Someone's got you and your phone. You say "Call my lawyer" (or whatever phrase you set).
- Phone looks normal
- But it's secretly alerting your contacts
- Can record audio too
- Attacker has no idea


6. Fall Detection
-----------------
Mainly for older folks or anyone at risk:
- Detects sudden falls
- Alerts your people
- Countdown to cancel false alarms
- Location shared
