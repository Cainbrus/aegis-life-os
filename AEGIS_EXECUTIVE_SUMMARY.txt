THE ONE-LINER
-------------
Aegis is a mobile security app that lets intruders into FAKE data while capturing their photo and location. Proactive protection, not reactive.


THE PROBLEM
-----------
Your phone is your entire life - bank, conversations, photos, health records, work. Current security is reactive: once someone gets in, damage is done. And what happens when someone FORCES you to unlock?


THE SOLUTION: TRAP MODE
-----------------------
Instead of locking attackers out, we let them in - to decoy data.

- Wrong pattern? They see fake messages, fake photos
- Under duress? Silent alert + fake data shown
- Meanwhile: Photo captured, location logged, actions recorded

They think they've won. You've got evidence.
